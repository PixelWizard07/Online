Detailed service scope

Exactly what is included and what we will do.

The final scope is agreed for each project, but this shows the practical work covered by AI & Automation.

✓

What’s included

  • Workflow audit and automation opportunity mapping
  • Trigger, condition and action logic for recurring processes
  • Integrations between supported tools, systems and data sources
  • Notifications, task routing, reminders and approval workflows
  • AI-assisted summarization, classification, drafting or support flows
  • Human review points for decisions that should not be fully automated
  • Monitoring logic and clear handling for failed or incomplete steps
↗

What work we will do

  • Study the current manual process and identify repeated work
  • Document inputs, rules, exceptions and desired outcomes
  • Design the automation flow from trigger to final action
  • Connect compatible tools and define how information moves between them
  • Add approval points and safeguards where human judgement is required
  • Use AI only where it creates a practical improvement in speed or quality
  • Test edge cases, failure states and refine the workflow using real scenarios
◎

What you receive

  • Automation workflow map
  • Trigger/action logic
  • Integration plan or implementation
  • AI use-case configuration
  • Approval and exception flow
  • Operational handover guidance

Frequently asked questions

Before starting AI & Automation.

What processes can be automated?

Repeated tasks with clear inputs, rules and outcomes are usually the strongest candidates.

Does automation remove all human involvement?

Not necessarily. Important reviews, approvals and exceptions can remain human-controlled.

Can automation connect with our existing tools?

Often yes, depending on the APIs and integration options those tools provide.
