Frequently asked questions

Before starting AI & Automation.

What processes can be automated?

Repeated tasks with clear inputs, rules and outcomes are usually the strongest candidates.

Does automation remove all human involvement?

Not necessarily. Important reviews, approvals and exceptions can remain human-controlled.

Can automation connect with our existing tools?

Often yes, depending on the APIs and integration options those tools provide.
