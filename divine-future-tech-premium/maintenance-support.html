Frequently asked questions

Before starting Maintenance & Support.

Can you maintain a site you did not originally build?

Often yes, after reviewing the existing structure, technology and access available.

Can maintenance include design improvements?

Yes. Support can include interface refinements and content improvements as well as technical fixes.

Is maintenance only for emergencies?

No. Planned improvements are usually healthier than waiting until problems become urgent.
